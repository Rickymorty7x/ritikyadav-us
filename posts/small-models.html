Why I'm bullish on small models

LLMs Efficiency Opinion

The headlines are about trillion-parameter behemoths. The interesting engineering, increasingly, is about how small you can go and still ship something useful.

Latency matters more than IQ

Most products live or die on a 200ms budget. A 7B model running locally with a quantized kernel can answer in 80ms. A frontier API at 800ms+ is invisible to the model but very visible to your user.

Privacy is a feature

"Send your medical records to a third party" is a hard sell. "Run this model on the device in your pocket" is not. The smaller the model, the more places it can live.

Fine-tuning becomes a craft, not a luxury

You can fine-tune a 3B model on a single consumer GPU in under an hour. That tightens the iteration loop in a way that changes how you think about the problem. You stop prompt-engineering around the model's weaknesses and start fixing them.

None of this means small > big in some absolute sense. It means the sweet spot for most real products is much smaller than the discourse suggests.
