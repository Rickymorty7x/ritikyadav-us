Building a RAG app in a weekend

RAG LangChain Side Projects

I had a folder full of ML papers I'd never properly read. The plan: build a RAG app that lets me chat with them over the weekend. The constraint: nothing more exotic than what fits in a single Python file.

The stack

  • LangChain for the glue.
  • FAISS for the vector store — local, fast, no infra.
  • Sentence-Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) for embeddings.
  • Streamlit for the UI, because anything more is procrastination.

What surprised me

The naive baseline — split PDFs into 1,000-character chunks with a 200-character overlap, embed everything, top-k=4 — was already shockingly good for the kind of question I was asking ("which paper introduced GroupNorm?", "summarise the BatchNorm vs LayerNorm trade-off").

What I'd redo

Two things:

  • Smarter chunking. Section-aware splitting beats character-count splitting once your queries get specific.
  • Reranking. A small cross-encoder reranker on the top 20 candidates dramatically improved answer quality at almost no latency cost.

Total time: ~14 hours over two evenings and a Sunday. Total lines of code: under 300. Total satisfaction: very high.
