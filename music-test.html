What each result tells us
  • Plays sound — the URL works in your browser/network. Use that one.
  • Loads but silent — file may be missing audio, or volume is muted at OS level.
  • 404 / Error — that exact filename doesn't exist on Wikimedia. Try the next.
  • CORS error in console — server blocks cross-origin audio. Try a different host or self-host.
  • "Video unavailable" on YouTube — embedding is restricted in your region or by uploader.
  • Nothing works — drop your own MP3 at audio/nepal-anthem.mp3 in the project folder.
